## Boiler design

Depending on their output, ZAO "Firma Galan" produces two groups of boilers, single-phase and three-phase:

- single-phase: "Ochag-2", "Ochag-3", "Ochag-5" and "Ochag-6";
- three-phase: "Geyser-9", "Geyser-15", "Vulkan-25", "Vulkan-36", "Vulkan-50".

**Single-phase boilers consist of:**

- a metal body with inlet and outlet connections for the heat-transfer fluid and a flange for mounting the electrode assembly. The body also serves as the second electrode and as the ionization chamber;
- an electrode assembly consisting of a flange with a rod electrode, a current lead and a hermetically sealed through-insulator. On the flange of single-phase boilers, at the bottom, there is a central terminal for the phase, and on the side a terminal for the neutral wire and a terminal for the earth wire;
- a protective cover.

**Three-phase boilers consist of:**

- a metal body with inlet and outlet connections for the heat-transfer fluid. The body also serves as the ionization chamber;
- a base on which 4 or 7 electrodes are mounted, depending on the boiler output, with current leads and hermetically sealed through-insulators;
- a terminal group with a protective cover.

The operating principle of all the boilers is the same and is described below.

## Advantages of electrode boilers

ZAO "Firma Galan" boilers are produced exclusively in Russia (8 models, Table 1). They are not only cheaper than boilers of all other types with comparable output, including foreign ones, but also have a number of very significant advantages:

Considerable energy savings thanks to the extremely high efficiency of electrode boilers, which reaches 98%, much higher than that of existing heating boilers of other types. This is achieved by converting electrical energy directly into heat in the heat-transfer fluid itself: as electric current passes through the fluid, the molecules are ionized, the temperature and conductivity of the fluid rise, the current flowing from electrode to electrode increases, and the boiler quickly reaches its rated output.

Savings of 20% to 40% of the energy consumed, thanks to the rapid response of electrode boilers and the use of automation: the electronic temperature controllers of ZAO "Firma Galan", "Navigator" and "Komfort", remote control over the cellular network, "Galan-GSM", and also the electromechanical version of the automation (MRT-15).

Simple installation, small dimensions and low weight of electrode boilers. For example, a boiler with an output of 6 kW weighs 1.65 kg, and one with 25 kW weighs 5.7 kg.

Our boilers can be built into previously installed heating systems (provided our recommendations are strictly followed), including as a second, backup boiler (parallel connection of the boiler), Fig. 3. The output of the backup boiler must match the size of the heated premises, the quality of their thermal insulation and the volume of heat-transfer fluid in the heating system (Table 1).

If a backup boiler is installed in an existing heating system, it is necessary to:

- thoroughly flush the system using the corrosion inhibitor "Galan-Protector";
- filter the heat-transfer fluid;
- take measures to prevent contaminants from the heating system from entering the boiler: install filters.

It is possible to multiply the output of the heating system many times over (in line with the increase in the volume of heated premises) by connecting a large number of boilers in parallel. For example, connecting 8 boilers of 25 kW each increases the output of the heating system to 200 kW (Fig. 2).

**Electrode boilers must not be used:**

- for "direct" water heating, when water from a pond, artesian well, well, water mains, river, etc. is fed "directly" into the boiler. Our boilers are intended for use in closed heating systems, in which the water circulates in closed loops. The water used as heat-transfer fluid must exactly match the nameplate data of the boiler, otherwise the boiler will fail. Water also may not be drawn off from the heating system for domestic hot water supply;
- for "warm floor" heating, since the temperatures required in this case are significantly lower than the optimal operating temperatures of an electrode boiler. For this reason the boiler does not reach its nameplate output. For "warm floors" we therefore recommend the highly reliable modern TEN-type (tubular electric heater) boilers Ochag-Turbo, Geyser-Turbo and Vulkan-Turbo, manufactured by ZAO "Firma Galan".

**Not recommended:**

- the use of electrode boilers in heating systems with large-volume radiators: radiators made of large-diameter pipes, cast-iron radiators, etc. In this case it is also recommended to use TEN-type boilers Ochag-Turbo, Geyser-Turbo or Vulkan-Turbo, manufactured by ZAO "Firma Galan".

If, however, due to circumstances you have no other option and you use an electrode boiler, the volume (quantity) of heat-transfer fluid filled into the system must match the output of the boiler (Table 1). In addition, if cast-iron radiators are used, a coarse filter and a mud trap (sediment separator) must be fitted in the "return" line, to prevent the situation,

**Designs for heating systems, installation of the boilers and automation, connection to the electricity network (including earthing), commissioning, repair, inspection of the condition and testing must be carried out by an organization (firm) that holds the appropriate license for such work.**

When carrying out the work listed above, preparing the documentation for the heating system, and operating it, the following must be observed:

- "Interindustry Rules for the Construction of Electrical Installations" (PUE, Electrical Installation Code);
- "Rules for the Technical Operation of Consumer Electrical Installations" (PTE);
- "Safety Rules for the Operation of Electrical Installations";
- "Rules for the Construction and Safe Operation of Electric Boilers and Electric Boiler Houses";

**Connecting the boilers, installing automatic control of the heating systems and their maintenance, including repair, require electricians or instrumentation and automation (KIPiA) specialists of appropriate qualification, certified and authorized to work on electrical installations up to 1000 V, with an electrical safety group of no less than 3. **Earthing.**

By the time the boiler is installed, the earthing of the heating system must already be fully completed and tested. The earthing resistance of the boiler and the heating system must not exceed

4 Ohm. Copper conductor with a cross-section of 4-6 mm² is used as the earthing conductor (Table No. 1).

Protective earthing must comply with the PUE and the "Rules for the Construction and Safe Operation of Electric Boilers and Electric Boiler Houses".

All exposed conductive parts of the boiler and the heating system, including the metal pipelines of the cold ("return") and hot heat-transfer fluid, must be earthed.

**Earthing electrodes**

Earthing electrodes must meet the requirements of the PUE.

The design and construction of the earthing electrode must ensure the required earthing resistance of the boiler and the heating system, not more than 4 Ohm.

The period for periodic inspection of the condition of the earthing electrode is 12 years.

Corrosion wear must not exceed 50%.

It is forbidden to paint the earthing structure or to protect it from corrosion with removable or permanent dielectric coatings, for example plastic or rubber sleeves or covers.

**Installation (mounting) of the boiler in the heating system.**

Before installation, inspect the boiler and check that it is complete.

The boiler is installed in the heating system strictly vertically. The terminal group (current leads of the boiler) for connection to the electricity network, protected from external influences and accidental contact by a protective cover (cap), must be located at the bottom. **The boiler connections are joined to the heating system pipelines by means of plumbing couplings with a DN not smaller than the DN of the boiler connections**

## Electrical wiring of the boilers (automation).

Three-phase boilers are connected to a 4-wire, three-phase electricity network with a voltage of 380 V and an industrial frequency of 50 Hz: N, neutral conductor, and PE, protective earth conductor. The N and PE conductors are separate. Single-phase boilers are connected to a single-phase network with a voltage of 220 V: N, neutral conductor, and PE, protective earth conductor. The N and PE conductors are separate. Make the connection to the electricity network according to the design of your heating system and in accordance with the automation system of ZAO "Firma Galan" that you have selected. **Observing "phase - neutral - earth" is mandatory.** All connecting wires must have the same cross-section, not less than that given in Table No. 1 (electromechanical version of the automation) for your boiler, or in the manual for the electronic heating system automation that you have selected.

## Filling the heating system with heat-transfer fluid

Your heating system is assembled. The boiler and the automatic control system are installed, and the system has been connected to the electricity network and earthed. There is no dirt or corrosion in the heating system. In this case the system is filled with heat-transfer fluid without preliminary flushing. The following conditions must be observed:

- the heating system must be disconnected from the electricity network during any filling of water or LF liquid into the system. Accidental connection to the electricity network must be ruled out;
- immediately after the heat-transfer fluid is poured into the system, check the system for tightness. Any leaks are not permissible.

Filling with the "Argus-Galan" LF liquid or with water is possible (Table No. 1).

Water as a heat-transfer fluid has a number of undoubted advantages, such as availability, low cost, environmental friendliness and good thermophysical properties. At the same time, water has drawbacks such as corrosion of the heating system, the possible need to adjust the specific electrical resistivity of the water during the heating season, and the need for annual repair of the boiler. Therefore, while not rejecting the use of water as a heat-transfer fluid, we recommend that you use the modern LF liquid "Argus-Galan" as the heat-transfer fluid.

### "Argus-Galan" LF liquid.

To increase the reliability and economic efficiency of the heating system, a universal low-freezing liquid, "Argus-Galan", with a freezing point of -40°C has been developed.

Special components have been added to the LF liquid to prevent foam and scale formation in the heating system, as well as components that dissolve existing scale and slow down the corrosion process.

The use of LF liquid protects the heating system against "freezing" in the event of a sudden power cut, and greatly simplifies the maintenance of the heating system, since its parameters and properties fully meet the requirements of a heating system with an electrode boiler. It also significantly improves the economic and operational indicators, including:

- commissioning work takes less time and is simpler;
- stable operation of the heating system is ensured;
- the costs of maintaining the system are reduced.

Filling the heating system with LF liquid.

There is no dirt or corrosion in the system.

A measured quantity of LF liquid is poured into the system through the drain valve using a "Malysh"-type pump or a similar device. Filling the expansion tank to 1/3 of its volume, after air has been bled from the system, is considered normal.

*If the measured starting (inrush) current is lower than the value given in Table No. 4 for your boiler, the starting current is corrected as follows:*

- prepare a solution of pure table salt: dissolve 1 teaspoon of salt in 200 g of hot water. This is the approximate quantity for 100 liters of water poured into the system. Depending on the difference between the measured starting current and its table value (Table No. 4) for your boiler and its output, and on the quantity of water in the system, determine the single dose of salt solution to be added to 20 liters of water drained from the system. Mix thoroughly. Using a "Malysh"-type pump, pour the mixture back into the system through the drain valve. Then drain another 20 liters from the system, without adding salt solution, and pour it back into the system. Run only the circulation pump for 20-30 minutes. The running time of the pump also depends on the size of the heating system and the volume of water in it. This is needed to equalize the water parameters throughout the whole volume of the heating system.

Then switch on the boiler and measure the starting (inrush) current 30 seconds after switching on, at a return temperature of 15°C-20°C. After the temperature at the "Inlet" sensor reaches 60°C, measure the maximum current and compare it with the value in Table No. 4. If necessary, carry out further adjustment of the water's specific electrical resistivity using the method above. Note that the starting current value is only indicative (a rough target) and serves to simplify the adjustment of the water's specific electrical resistivity. The final criterion is the equality of the boiler's maximum current, when the temperature at the boiler inlet (Inlet sensor) reaches 60°C, with the value given in Table No. 4, after which the resistivity adjustment is complete.

## Switching on the boiler

Switch on the boiler, and keep the closest possible watch on the condition of the system until the first automatic switch-offs and switch-ons of the boiler.

It is not permissible to switch on the boiler if there is frozen heat-transfer fluid in the system or a risk of it freezing.

During commissioning, the temperature of the room in which the heating system is installed must be at least 15÷20°C.

If the heating system and the electrical wiring diagram, including the automation, are correctly installed, the system will begin to warm up.

If, in the initial period of operation, with "fresh" water as the heat-transfer fluid, the boiler's output gradually decreases, it is necessary to:

- remove the boiler from the system, disassemble it and clean the electrode surfaces and the inner surfaces of the boiler from the layer of deposits with insulating (dielectric) properties that has built up on them, then reassemble the boiler and install it in the system.

## Preventive maintenance and repair work. Flushing the system

The purpose of this work is to ensure the reliable, efficient and long-term operation of the heating system. The work is carried out by qualified personnel, and the heating system must be disconnected from the electricity network.

**Preventive maintenance and repair work is carried out by a firm (organization) that holds the appropriate license for such work. The entry on the work performed is made by the contractor in the register of this manual and is certified by the signature of the person responsible for the work and the stamp of the organization. (Appendix No. 2)**

Preventive maintenance and repair work is carried out after the heating season. Its frequency and content are determined both by the type of heat-transfer fluid used in the heating system and by the defects identified during operation, inspection of the condition of the system and boiler, and repair.

**Heat-transfer fluid: water**

Every year, immediately after the end of the heating season, it is necessary to:

- cool the heating system down to room temperature;
- drain 20 liters of water from the system and dissolve in this quantity the dose of the corrosion inhibitor "Galan-Protector". The dose is determined at the rate of 1.5 liters (or 0.5 liters, depending on the concentration) of inhibitor per 100 liters of water. Mix thoroughly, then pour it into the system through the drain valve using a "Malysh"-type pump. Drain another 20 liters of water from the system and pour it back in without inhibitor, and so on until the system is completely filled with water containing the inhibitor.

## Boiler repair

Dismantle the boiler from the heating system if it is otherwise impossible to remove the electrode group (electrode) from the boiler body, and also clean the inner surfaces of the body and make sure there are no defects.

During boiler repair it is necessary to:

• remove the electrode (for a single-phase boiler) or the electrode group (for a three-phase boiler) and inspect it for defects, such as cracks in the through-insulators, and determine the degree of electrode wear. If wear exceeds 40%, the electrode or electrodes must be replaced. This usually happens once every 3-5 years;

---

<!-- page 32 -->

• carefully clean the mating surfaces to ensure tightness, using sealing materials when assembling the boiler and during its further operation;

• clean the inner surfaces of the boiler body to a metallic shine and then carefully inspect them for defects, for example pitting corrosion;

• assemble the boiler. When assembling, strictly observe the alignment of the electrodes with each other and with the body, that is, the distance (gap) between the electrodes and the body, and between the electrodes, must be the same. Prevent any contamination from getting inside the boiler during assembly. Check for tightness.

Install the boiler strictly vertically in the heating system. Fill the heating system with heat-transfer fluid. The expansion tank must be filled to 1/3 of its volume.

After completing the work listed above, check the system for tightness, tighten the bolted and electrical connections of the boiler and automation, check the reliability of the earth connection and the earthing resistance, and carry out a trial start-up of the heating system. Rectify any defects identified, if there are any.

The person responsible for this work makes the corresponding entry in the Register of repair work performed, certifies it with their signature and the stamp of the firm (organization), stating its address and license (Appendix No. 2).

**Heat-transfer fluid: LF liquid "Argus-Galan".**

In this case, preventive maintenance and repair of the boiler during the off-season is carried out once every 3 years. This consists of an inspection, checking the tightness of the bolted and electrical connections, and checking the reliability of the earth connection.

## Visual monitoring of boiler operation

This function may be performed by persons not younger than 18 years old who have received briefing on monitoring the boiler's operation, know the design of the boiler and have studied this manual.

When monitoring the boiler's operation, visually check the heat-transfer fluid level in the expansion tank, which indicates the amount of fluid in the system. Normally the tank should be filled to 1/3 of its volume. If the level of heat-transfer fluid in the expansion tank is below this, the specialist of the firm (organization) servicing the system must carry out one of two procedures, depending on whether water or low-freezing liquid is used as the heat-transfer fluid:

a). Heat-transfer fluid: water.

Top up the expansion tank to the normal level with distilled water, or clean, filtered rain or melted snow water, that is, water with a specific electrical resistivity significantly above 3100 Ohm cm.

b). Heat-transfer fluid: low-freezing liquid.

## Annual work ensuring electrical safety and reliability of automation equipment

Every year, after the end of the heating season, work must be carried out to ensure the electrical safety and reliability of the automation equipment, regardless of whether water or LF liquid is used as the heat-transfer fluid (carried out with the system disconnected from the electricity network):

• check the reliability of the contacts of the earthing system and of the automation unit;

• check the electrical resistance of the heating system's earthing. It must not exceed 4 Ohm;

• inspect the circuit breaker: clean off dust, check and tighten the terminal connections, check for mechanical sticking and check the contacts;

• check the magnetic starter: clean off dust, inspect, clean and tighten the terminal connections, check the condition of the contacts for mechanical sticking and magnetic adhesion;

• inspect the start buttons;

• inspect the installed automation equipment ("Istopnik", "Navigator", "Galan-GSM"): wipe off dust, check the contacts, the wiring and serviceability.

**The above work is carried out by electricians or KIPiA (instrumentation and automation) fitters authorized to work on electrical installations up to 1000 V, with an electrical safety group of no less than 3. After this work is completed, a trial start-up of the boiler is carried out. After the work is finished, the specialist responsible for it makes the corresponding entry in the Register of work performed, certifies it with their signature and the stamp of the firm (organization), stating its address and license (Appendix No. 2).**

## Fire safety

When carrying out installation and commissioning work, and when operating the electrode boiler, the "Fire Safety Rules of the Russian Federation" must be observed, including the following.

**Prohibited:**

• storing in the room where the boiler is installed cylinders of liquefied or compressed gas, flammable liquids, flammable materials, or fuel and lubricant materials, for example petrol, acetone, turpentine, bitumen, soft roofing, kerosene, lubricating oils, etc.;

• drying clothes on the boiler.

## Transport and storage of electrode boilers

1. The boiler must not have any damage and must remain operational after mechanical and climatic influences during transport.

2. Boilers may be transported by all types of transport in covered vehicles, in accordance with the rules for carriage of goods applicable to that type of transport. Transport conditions with respect to mechanical factors: group C per GOST 23216-78.

3. Transport conditions with respect to climatic factors: group 5 (OZh 4) per GOST 15150-69.

4. Storage conditions for the boilers: group 3 (ZhZ) per GOST 15150-69.
